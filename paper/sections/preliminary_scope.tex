\par\smallskip\noindent\textbf{When is collaboration useful?}
The relevant question is whether the VLM can repair the specialist's errors without undoing its correct answers. A stronger average VLM can still harm individual examples, while a weaker one may offer complementary strengths. Useful routing therefore requires both replacement opportunities and pre-call signals that identify them. We distinguish two decisions: estimating paired outcomes during training, and ranking inputs at deployment. Four-state supervision and post-hoc deferral provide alternative implementations of this shared decision. Comparing fixed-weight scores helps separate the effect of ranking from the information supplied by supervision.
